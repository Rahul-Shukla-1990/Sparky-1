# Clusters, Reports and Attachments — v0.7

## Incident Mini-Maps

Each incident card displays a mini-map if latitude and longitude are available. If coordinates are missing, the card shows a location-not-plotted note.

## Clusters

Clusters are analyst-created groupings such as piracy spikes, IUU patterns, pollution trends, chokepoint events, cyber events, vessel-linked cases or report baskets.

## Report Inclusion Flags

Each incident can be independently marked for Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Half-Yearly and Annual reports.

## Report Graphs

The Reports tab shows cluster-wise, category-wise and alert-level graphs for the selected reporting period. Only incidents marked for that period are counted.

## Attachments

Each incident can carry supporting links, images, screenshots, PDFs, Word files and other local documents. Files are stored under `data/uploads` and served through `/uploads/<filename>`.
